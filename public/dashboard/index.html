Personal Information

Data Deletion & Privacy

According to Cyber Netra's data privacy compliance guides, you have absolute authority to purge files and delete analysis reports permanently from our cache directories.

Data Security & Privacy Controls

Cyber Netra prioritizes information security and personal data confidentiality. This portal is aligned with ISO 27001:2022 cybersecurity guidelines.

Data Masking

Phone numbers, transactional details and emails are masked in the portal views to block credential leak vectors.

Automated Purging

Evidence uploads are deleted immediately after forensic scoring completes, unless explicitly attached to a submitted report.

No Personal Logs Selling

Cyber Netra is a safety service. We never sell, log or utilize citizen profile searches for commercial monetization.

Help & Support Desk

If you have encountered issues using the AI profile checks, face/media verification matching, or report creations, review the guidelines below.

How long does media deepfake analysis take?

Usually takes 10 to 30 seconds depending on frame resolution size and video codecs. Do not reload the page during analysis.

Will Cyber Netra report my findings to the police automatically?

No. We provide risk indexes. You must explicitly create an incident statement in the 'My Reports' step flow to log details.
